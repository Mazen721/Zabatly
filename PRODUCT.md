# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Four user types, all primarily mobile, browsing in a relaxed state:

- **Renters** (primary): Looking for vehicles casually, comparing options, booking when something fits. Not in a rush; they want to feel confident in their choice.
- **Vehicle Owners**: Listing their vehicles for rental income. Need clarity on bookings, earnings, and vehicle status.
- **Drivers**: Offering their driving services to renters. Need to manage availability and see incoming requests.
- **Admins**: Managing the platform, verifying users (KYC/OCR), handling disputes, monitoring fleet health.

Context: Egyptian and Arab market. Users range from students and young professionals to families and business travelers. They expect mobile-first, familiar patterns, and zero friction.

## Product Purpose

Zabatly ("زبطلي", Egyptian Arabic for "fix it for me") is a smart vehicle rental and transportation platform. It connects renters with vehicle owners and drivers, using AI to recommend the best match based on preferences, and OCR for identity verification.

Success looks like: the user finishes a booking and thinks "that was surprisingly easy, like a smart friend just sorted it out for me." The platform handles complexity so the user never feels it.

## Brand Personality

**Reliable, Friendly, Confident.**

- Reliable: always delivers, no surprises, trustworthy. Like a friend who never flakes.
- Friendly: warm, approachable, never cold or corporate. Speaks simply, not down.
- Confident: knows what it's doing. No hedging, no clutter, no "are you sure?" anxiety. The interface leads.

Supporting qualities: premium polish (high craft, not cheap-looking) and local cultural resonance (built for Egypt, speaks the user's language without being gimmicky about it).

## Anti-references

- **Boring/generic startup templates.** Cookie-cutter SaaS layouts with stock photography, identical card grids, and "Get Started Free" hero sections. Zabatly should be immediately distinguishable.
- **Childish/cartoonish interfaces.** Rounded-everything, mascot characters, excessive illustrations, bright candy colors. Zabatly is friendly, not juvenile.
- **Government/bureaucratic UI.** Stiff forms, formal language, dated layouts, walls of text. Zabatly is casual and modern, not an e-government portal.
- **Generic ride-hailing clones.** Zabatly is not an Uber/Careem skin. It's a rental and transportation platform with its own identity.

## Design Principles

1. **"Zabatly handles it."** Every interaction should feel like the platform is doing the heavy lifting. AI recommendations, smart defaults, pre-filled forms. The user decides; Zabatly executes.
2. **Trust through clarity.** Transparent pricing, real vehicle photos, verified driver profiles, clear booking status. Confidence comes from never wondering what's happening.
3. **Familiar before smart.** AI features should feel natural and expected, not showy or gimmicky. Like autocomplete: useful the moment you notice it, invisible when you don't need it.
4. **Local roots, global polish.** Egyptian character with international design quality. The vibe of Airbnb's warmth, Revolut's precision, and WhatsApp's "just works" effortlessness, built for the Egyptian market.
5. **Speed is respect.** Fast load times, minimal steps to book, no hunting for information. The interface respects users' time.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- No specific WCAG target yet; focus on general usability: keyboard navigation, adequate contrast, readable text sizes.
- Bilingual: English (primary) and Arabic. RTL support planned.
- Reduced motion preferences should be respected (moderate animation energy by default, with `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback).

## Voice & Tone

Casual English. Friendly, conversational, simple words. Not corporate-speak, not slang-heavy. Like texting a reliable friend who happens to know everything about cars.

## Motion Energy

Moderate. Noticeable transitions, page animations, hover effects. Enough to feel alive and responsive, not enough to distract or slow things down.

## References

- **Airbnb**: warm browsing experience, discovery-oriented, trust-building through transparency.
- **Revolut**: modern, slick, confident UI. Premium feel without pretension.
- **WhatsApp**: effortless, familiar, "everyone uses it without thinking" simplicity.
